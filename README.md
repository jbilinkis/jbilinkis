# Julia Bilinkis

**Product Strategy · AI-Native Product Management · Agent Harness Design**

I help product leaders design AI-native ways of working, from product strategy and decision-making to agent architecture, context engineering, and human–AI collaboration.

I’m the founder of [StrategicMOVE.education](https://strategicmove.education), a product strategy consultant, educator, author, and speaker. My background includes more than 15 years in fintech and telecom leadership, product consulting, and executive education. I wrote *Управление продуктом. Российская практика*.

## What I work on

| Focus | What I design and teach |
| --- | --- |
| **Product Strategy** | Business models, discovery, metrics, and decision-making. |
| **Agent Harness** | How to structure context, choose between workflows and agent architectures, and design tools, permissions, and evaluations. |

## In development

**Agent Harness Engineering for Product Managers** is my current focus. I’m developing open resources with templates, worked examples, and exercises on three topics:

- **Agent Harness for Product Managers** covers context, tool permissions, and human review.
- **Context-to-Strategy Engine** explores how to turn product research and data into strategic options.
- **Agent Evaluation Playbook** covers quality criteria, test cases, and failure analysis.
